Gödel, Escher, Bach, explained

GEB

Interactive, visual, sound-on explainers for Douglas Hofstadter's Gödel, Escher, Bach: an Eternal Golden Braid, added a dialogue and chapter at a time. Every new word is explained where it first appears.

The book alternates Dialogues (stories that show an idea) with Chapters (which explain it), so each page here covers a dialogue together with the chapter that follows it. Summaries are original paraphrases; no text from the book is reproduced. Music is synthesized live in your browser.
